About

I am an applied finance theorist working on topics related to financial intermediation. My research studies how financial intermediaries affect the stability and efficiency of the financial system, with a focus on liquidity provision, regulation, and information frictions in banking and financial markets.

Before joining NYU Stern, I was an Assistant Professor of Finance at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from New York University and my undergraduate and master’s degrees in Economics from Universidad Torcuato Di Tella.

Research Interests

Financial intermediation, liquidity provision, regulation, information frictions, specialized lending, price informativeness, and stress testing.
